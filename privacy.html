Our Universal Privacy Principles

  • Local Storage Priority: All applications developed by NovaByte Lab store personal logs, expense numbers, and health records strictly in local on-device SQLite databases.
  • No Account Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or device IDs to advertising brokers.
  • Direct Data Deletion: Deleting an entry inside any NovaByte Lab application, clearing app cache in Android Settings, or uninstalling an app irreversibly destroys all stored records.
