1. Overview & Health Data Protection

NovaByte Lab treats your personal health records with the highest privacy standard. VitalPulse is designed for individuals seeking to track blood pressure, pulse, mean arterial pressure (MAP), and blood glucose levels. All measurements are saved 100% locally on your smartphone and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

2. Nature of Data Stored

VitalPulse records measurements entered manually by the user, including systolic and diastolic blood pressure, heart rate, blood glucose readings (mg/dL or mmol/L), meal tags, and medicine schedules. This sensitive biometric information never leaves your device.

3. Medical Disclaimer

VitalPulse is a personal health logging tool for informational purposes only. It is not a medical device, cannot take physical blood pressure readings by itself, and does not replace professional medical diagnosis or clinical treatment.

4. Data Portability & Doctor Reports

VitalPulse allows users to generate local clinical PDF summaries for personal use or to share directly with their physician. These PDF files are generated on-device and are shared only when the user explicitly triggers Android's native share menu.
