1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how NovaByte Lab ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy for the SubVexo mobile application. SubVexo is designed with a strict offline-first privacy framework: all financial records, subscription costs, and billing cycles remain on your device and are never uploaded to remote servers.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

Offline SQLite Storage: All subscription details (names, renewal dates, costs, categories, notes) are stored strictly inside your phone's private application sandbox using SQLite. NovaByte Lab has zero access to your subscriptions.

Zero Bank Credentials: SubVexo never requests or connects to your bank account, credit/debit card numbers, or online banking passwords.

3. Advertisements & Google Services

SubVexo integrates verified Google developer SDKs:

  • Google AdMob: The free tier displays advertisements. Google AdMob may collect non-precise diagnostic identifiers in accordance with Google Advertising Policies. SubVexo Pro eliminates all ads permanently.
  • Google Play In-App Review API: Allows users to submit native Google Play ratings directly within the app without transmitting personal data to NovaByte Lab.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases for SubVexo Pro are processed directly by Google. We never see or store your payment details.

4. Android Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used locally to trigger upcoming renewal reminders and trial conversion alerts via Android AlarmManager.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC: Used locally to lock the app. Fingerprint or Face ID data is evaluated entirely by your phone's hardware security chip and is never accessible to the app.

5. Data Deletion and Portability

You can delete subscriptions, export manual JSON backups, or clear app data in Android Settings to immediately and permanently erase all stored records.
